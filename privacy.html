Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Number Rush.

Data Collection

Number Rush does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information. The game does not require an account, login, email address, phone number, or profile.

Analytics And Tracking

Number Rush does not use third-party analytics, advertising trackers, cross-app tracking, or tracking identifiers.

Local Game Data

The game may save progress, settings, unlocked levels, stars, and coins locally on your device. This information stays on your device and is used only to make the game work.

Children's Privacy

Number Rush does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Data Sharing

Because Number Rush does not collect personal information, there is no personal information to sell, rent, disclose, or share with third parties.

Internet Access

Number Rush is designed for offline play. If a future version adds online features, this policy will be updated before those features are released.

Changes To This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the game changes. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the App Store product page.
